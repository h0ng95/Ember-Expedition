## Legal progression

Four starters × seven final-evolution/combination policies × three research paths × two growth orders × two seeds = **336 routes**. All use production APIs, actual earned gold, normal rewards and the normal three-chance limit. Research starts at wave 11, at most one purchase per preparation. The fixed policy uses spread placement and switches to compact when two chances remain. No grants or forced wins advance a route.

At the first naturally reached waves 20–30, snapshot the current party. Separately replay that identical party against the previous/next encounter and with compact/spread placement. These isolated fights never feed rewards or state back into the legal route. Raw `legal.json` distinguishes **6,253 actual fights**, **3,938 isolated fights** and **1,004 anchors** for the archived seeds. Successful routes: 65/336.

## Equal-investment matrix

Nine anchor builds (F-AR/F-FI/F-IC and H-01…H-06) × three companion backlines × three material budgets × six research sets × two layouts × eleven encounters = **10,692 fights**.

- All five units are deployed. Materials include consumed growth/combination/trait donors, not only surviving units.
- Budgets are 23, 27 and 35 recruited copies. Research costs exactly 140 gold in each case; no permanent research bonuses.
- A final anchor costs seven copies; a hybrid costs six. The hybrid's saved copy buys a legal banner-trait transfer to the frontline knight. It is not spent on a partial growth point with no current combat benefit.
- The frontline is always F-KN. Backline 0 uses priest/fire/ice, backline 1 priest/archer/banner, backline 2 archer/priest/banner. At 23 copies only the anchor and frontline are fully developed; at 27 the first support also is; at 35 all non-hybrid units are final forms.
- Branches are fixed: guard knight, piercing archer, healing priest, burning fire mage, freezing ice mage, guarding banner. This is not an exhaustive search of all branches, companions, traits or placements.
- The equivalent gold field is `(copies - 3) × 20 + 140` (540/620/780), accounting for three starting units. This is a material-budget comparison, **not the actual random recruitment cost of assembling a chosen party**. Acquisition order, unwanted recruits and bench waste are absent from this matrix.
- A production encounter supplies the actual enemy HP, attack, defense, interval, range, speed, role, boss pattern flag and positions. Only the fixture's wave index is moved; it generates no progression rewards.
- `Step` uses the production battle engine's internal 1/60-second ticks and a 75-second limit. No scene simulation or damage approximation is substituted.

The original exploratory 7,128-fight matrix used only 23/27 copies and an ineffective partial-growth sink for the one-copy difference. It was superseded and is **not included** in the final report. Final legal output came from `.local-tools/v068-lategame/legal.json`; final controlled output came from `.local-tools/v068-controlled-final/controlled.json`.

Percentages are deterministic scenario coverage, **not human win probabilities**. Routes share seeds and policies, adjacent snapshots are correlated, and late-game arrivals are selected survivors. Compare identical-party pairs before attributing an effect to encounter strength. Do not infer a hybrid's strength directly from its legal-route completion count: acquisition time and the fixed investment policy also differ.

## Retry regression and device checks

```powershell
& tests/CatalogBattleReport/run.ps1
```

Unity entry point: `Expedition.EditorTools.RetryReviewValidation.ValidateAndBuild`. It consumes legal fixtures generated by:

```powershell
& tests/MidgamePressure/run.ps1 -FirstSeed 67501 -Count 2 -SelectedOnly -Output '.local-tools/v067-holdout'
```

The Unity validator checks exact ally IDs (including duplicate forms), resource/RNG/undo preservation, repeated selection, the ordinary retry button, missing/stale records, victory and exhausted-run guards. It exports four fixtures under `unity/Logs/v068-fixtures` and builds Android.

`MidgameReportAudit` remains development-only. An explicit one-shot `midgame-report-audit.flag` containing `retry` renders 18 result/report/focus/support/fallback frames. `retry-manual` waits for actual taps: result report → first lost ally → previous report → third-row healer. It blocks storage writes and restores original screen state in `finally`. Save/PlayerPrefs backup and byte-for-byte restore are required around device QA.
